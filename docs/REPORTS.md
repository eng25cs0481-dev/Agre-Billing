# Agre Billing — Reports & Registers

## 1. Accounting & Financial Reports

1. **Day Book**: Chronological audit register of all daily vouchers with debit/credit breakdown, status badges, and period filters.
2. **Ledger Statement**: Account statement for individual customer, supplier, cash, bank, or expense accounts showing opening balance, transaction particular, debit, credit, and running balance.
3. **Cash Book & Bank Book**: Detailed cash inflow/outflow register and bank transactions.
4. **Sales Register & Purchase Register**: Periodic summary of all billed sales and inbound purchases.
5. **Outstanding Receivables & Payables**: Customer dues and supplier payables with credit limits and net balances.
6. **Stock Summary & Valuation**: Quantity on hand, cost rate, selling rate, low-stock indicators, and total inventory value ($Qty \times Cost$).
7. **Expense Report**: Categorized expense analysis by account head.
